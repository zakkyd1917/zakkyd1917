# Systems Architecture & Institutional Analysis

## About

I am a systems architect with interests spanning information systems, cybersecurity, infrastructure, compliance, and applied research.

My work centers on understanding how complex systems are designed, how their components interact, and how technical and institutional decisions affect their reliability, security, and accountability.

I am particularly interested in the intersection of technology and human affairs: how information is collected, organized, interpreted, and acted upon, and how systems can be developed to make those processes more transparent, rigorous, and useful.

I approach systems architecture as both a technical discipline and a means of examining the institutions, processes, and decisions that increasingly depend upon technological infrastructure.

My current projects include security and abuse-reporting infrastructure, automated research and media-analysis tools, and independent publishing systems.

I favor practical engineering, careful documentation, evidence-based analysis, and architectures that remain understandable and maintainable long after their initial implementation.

## Areas of Interest

- **Systems Architecture:** Infrastructure design, integration, reliability, and maintainability.
- **Information Security:** Cybersecurity, abuse prevention, information integrity, and operational resilience.
- **Institutional Systems:** Governance, compliance, accountability, and the interaction between technological and organizational structures.
- **Research Infrastructure:** Media analysis, evidence preservation, information retrieval, and comparative research.
- **Automation:** Practical tools that improve consistency, transparency, and operational efficiency.
